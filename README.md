 Coffee Shop Sales Analysis | Microsoft Excel

 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes coffee shop sales data using **Microsoft Excel** to understand sales performance, customer footfall, product demand, peak ordering hours, and store-level performance.

🎯 Business Problem

The coffee shop needs to understand its sales and customer ordering patterns across different products, store locations, hours, weekdays, and order sizes.

The analysis focuses on identifying:

* Peak ordering hours
* Best-performing product categories
* Store locations with stronger sales and footfall
* Customer size preferences
* Top and low-performing products

🎯 Objectives

* Analyze total sales and customer footfall
* Identify peak and low-performing hours
* Compare sales and footfall across store locations
* Analyze sales by product category
* Understand customer preferences by order size
* Identify top-performing products

🛠️ Tools & Skills

**Microsoft Excel**

* Data Cleaning
* Pivot Tables
* Pivot Charts
* Data Analysis
* Data Visualization
* Business Insights

 🧹 Data Cleaning

The dataset was prepared before analysis by:

* Checking for duplicate records
* Removing duplicate values
* Identifying and handling null values
* Reviewing the data for consistency

 📊 Analysis Performed

1. Orders by Hour

Analyzed order quantity across different hours of the day.

Insight:Orders were stronger during the morning hours and declined after 10 AM.

 2. Sales by Product Category

Analyzed the percentage contribution of different product categories to overall sales.

Insight: Coffee and tea generated strong sales, while coffee beans had comparatively lower sales.

3. Sales & Footfall by Store Location

Compared sales and customer footfall across different store locations.

Insight: Hell's Kitchen recorded stronger sales and footfall, while Lower Manhattan showed comparatively lower performance.

4. Order Size Distribution

Analyzed customer orders based on product size.

Insight:Regular-size orders were the most popular, while small-size orders had comparatively lower demand.

 5. Product Sales Performance

Analyzed sales performance across individual products.

Insight: Barista Espresso was one of the best-performing products, while Brewed Black Tea showed comparatively lower sales.

💡 Key Business Insights

* Morning hours showed stronger order activity.
* Coffee and tea were major contributors to sales.
* Store performance varied by location.
* Regular-size products were preferred by customers.
* Product performance varied significantly across the menu.
* Low-performing products and locations represent potential areas for further analysis.

📈 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the coffee shop could:

* Introduce promotions during lower-demand hours.
* Investigate reasons for lower-performing store locations.
* Promote high-performing products through combo offers and upselling.
* Review pricing and positioning of low-performing products.
* Optimize inventory and staffing based on customer ordering patterns.

✅ Conclusion
The analysis helped identify peak sales periods, customer preferences, high-performing store locations, and product-level opportunities. These insights can support better decisions around sales strategies, marketing campaigns, inventory planning, and store operations.

The insights can support better decisions related to **sales, promotions, inventory, staffing, and store performance**.
